How Seasonal Forecasting Works: 3-Step Guide

Comparing what sold during this month last year against current stock to restock before you run out
Step 1: Check Last Year Demand Same Month History

What the system does: Reads units sold during this exact calendar month last year (e.g. November 2025).

Last Year Demand = Real sales volume in same month
Example: 44 boxes sold last November
Captures seasonal surges (flu, rainy weather, fever epidemics).
Step 2: Compare Shelf Stock Depletion Risk

What the system does: Compares current units on shelf against last year's proven monthly demand.

Depletion Risk = Current Stock vs. Last Year Demand
Example: 8 on shelf vs. 44 sold = High Risk (Restock!)
Immediately alerts you before your shelves run empty.
Step 3: Calculate Reorder Suggested PO

What the system does: Adds supplier safety cushion and subtracts current stock.

To Order = (Last Year Demand + Safety Cushion) − Stock
Example: (44 + 9 buffer) − 8 on shelf = 45 boxes to order
Ensures sufficient stock plus safety buffer for distributor lead time.
Why Year-Over-Year Seasonal Forecasting is Best for Retail Pharmacies Medicine consumption is strongly seasonal. Cough and cold syrups spike during the rainy/cool season, antibiotics surge following weather shifts, and vitamins peak around school and holiday months. Comparing current shelf stock directly against last year's same-month demand provides clear, actionable restock guidance without complicated guesswork.
